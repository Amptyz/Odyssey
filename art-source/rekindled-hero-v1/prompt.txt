Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a polished original pixel-art character DESIGN SHEET for Project Ember, concept preview only, not a sliced runtime sprite atlas.
Primary request: redesign the neutral playable protagonist as an ADULT nameless corpse reignited by a single surviving ember. In this world an ancient hero stole fire and brought a human golden age; the fire was extinguished and darkness returned. A tiny spark revived several dead bodies, now driven by hunger for fire and burning. The protagonist is one of these formerly ordinary dead people, NOT the ancient hero, not a chosen knight or a preset class.
Style/medium: premium meticulously authored PIXEL ART throughout. Clearly visible deliberate square pixel clusters, selective crisp outlines, limited coherent palette, 3-4 stepped tones per material, strong sculpted silhouette and expressive posing. Rich and beautiful through placement of clusters and controlled material contrast, never through photographic texture, smooth painting, soft gradients, blur, bloom or a pixelation filter. Adult anatomy, compact approximately 4.5-head-tall proportions; grounded dark fantasy, not cute chibi, not anime.
Composition: a landscape 3:2 game-art direction board with generous spacing. The left 42% contains one impressive large complete full-body THREE-QUARTER FRONT neutral-standing key design. The middle 28% contains two smaller complete versions of the EXACT same adult character: front and rear three-quarter studies, same anatomy, hair, bandages, clothing, burn scars and colors. The right 30% contains two intimate pixel-art detail insets (face and upper-chest ember scar), a small in-world readability vignette of this same character walking over moderately lit ashen cemetery stone, and a restrained row of eight color swatches. All feet and hands fully shown; silhouettes separated; no overlap. Sparse precise English headings only: "EMBER", "THE REKINDLED", "BASE BODY / CONCEPT 01", "TURNAROUND", "DETAILS", "GAME SCALE". No paragraphs or pretend annotations.
Character design: an adult androgynous lean weathered human with long enough limbs for expressive action, slightly forward intent posture, barefoot-looking wrapped feet with plain dark worn soles. Ash-pale grey skin with muted mauve shadows; visibly dead, gaunt cheeks, dark eye sockets, tiny amber-coal pupils. Short uneven soot-dark hair partly singed and ash-grey at the edges; not a sculpted fashionable hairstyle. One thin irregular dark-red healed cheek cut and restrained exposed charred scarring. Simple collarless smoke-grey burial tunic, sleeves torn around upper arms, short split hem above knees, a plain tied waist cloth, dull linen forearm and calf wrappings and dark worn close-fitting trousers. Fabric holes and missing fabric are a few deliberate large shapes, no shaggy noise. A diagonal tear in the tunic exposes a small jagged smoldering fissure at the sternum: dull reddish charred edges and one concentrated orange ember core, NO neon all-over body cracks or generic glowing circular reactor. Dark dried blood in the cloth and a few fresh red traces on knuckles and the exposed wound create a visceral bloody mood, with non-photorealistic stylized damage. Hands human, five fingers, no claws. No exposed internal organs; no severed parts.
Identity and equipment constraints: this is the unequipped shared base body. NO weapons, armor, metal shoulder pads, shield, crown, horned helmet, hood, cape, long wizard robe, jewelry, class emblems or elaborate runes. Leave head, torso and hands available for later interchangeable equipment. No full-body permanent fire aura. Blood and ash are intentional readable patches, not a noisy coating. Keep recognizably human melancholy and a hungry forward gaze, not an evil monster caricature.
Lighting/palette: grim and blood-marked but VISUALLY LEGIBLE, NOT underexposed. Main design sheet uses a medium-light desaturated grey-olive parchment ground, understated dark charcoal dividers, bone-white skin highlights, ash-grey linen, charcoal contours, muted violet shadows, dried oxblood, brick red fresh blood, concentrated amber and warm ivory fire. Face, fingers, fabric planes and wrapped feet readable without zoom. In-world vignette uses cool medium-value ruin stone and a slightly darker environment; preserve clear value separation of actor and ground. No black void backgrounds, pastel fantasy foliage or bright cheerful greens. Ember glow occupies a small local region with stepped pixel colors.
Quality constraints: consistent character in every view, exactly two arms/two legs, no duplicated hands, complete silhouette, deliberate clean pixel edges, strong negative space, beautiful restrained graphic layout. This is a concept board to choose a visual identity, not a claim of finished animation or a production sprite sheet. No existing franchise characters, logos or artist imitation.
